// Our Mission

Empowering Farmers
Through Neural Intelligence

WheatGuard is an AI-driven agricultural diagnostic platform engineered to address one of the most critical challenges facing wheat cultivation across South Asia and global wheat belts — the early identification and targeted management of crop diseases that silently devastate harvests each season.

Wheat is a cornerstone of food security for billions of people. Yet every year, unchecked fungal infections, blights, and rots claim devastating portions of the harvest — often because early microscopic signs go undetected until irreversible damage has occurred. Our platform exists to change that reality.

By combining state-of-the-art ConvNeXt neural network architectures with an intuitive field-ready interface, WheatGuard enables farmers, agronomists, and agricultural extension workers to diagnose diseases like Black Point, Fusarium Foot Rot, Leaf Blight, and Wheat Blast with a single photograph taken directly from the field.

Our mission is to democratize precision agriculture — delivering laboratory-grade crop diagnostics to anyone with a phone or drone, protecting harvests and farmer livelihoods.

🌾 Early Detection

Identify fungal pathogens at early incubation stages before widespread spore dispersal. Days saved in diagnosis mean the difference between a bountiful harvest and total crop failure.

🤖 AI Diagnostics

Our convolutional neural network is trained on extensively annotated regional wheat pathology datasets, calibrated with 99%+ validation accuracy for high specificity.

🚁 Drone Integration

WheatGuard supports multi-spectral drone video analysis — enabling automated field-scale canopy surveillance, GPS-tagged infection mapping, and early hotspot detection.

📊 Actionable Insights

Every scan delivers full probability distributions, pathogen taxonomy, severity assessments, and specific chemical and cultural agronomic treatment recommendations.
